# Privacy policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy explains how Lapsha, a personal notebook about the people who matter to you, handles information. It covers the mobile app and this website.

## Your information in Lapsha

Names, facts, important dates, photos you choose, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. Lapsha uses this information to display and organize your personal notebook. The app does not require an account and does not send your saved notebook to the developer or a developer-operated server.

Lapsha does not include advertising or advertising trackers. We do not sell your personal information or use your notebook for advertising. The app uses Expo Observe for performance monitoring and error diagnostics, as described below.

## Photos

When you choose a photo using the system photo picker, Lapsha stores a resized copy in its local app storage. Choosing or removing a photo in Lapsha does not change the original in your photo library.

## System backups and device transfers

Depending on your device settings, eligible app data may be included in backups or device transfers managed by Apple, Google, or your device provider. These services operate under their own privacy policies and settings. The developer cannot access or manage your system backups.

On iOS, Lapsha uses the system’s standard backup behavior, which may include saved records, preferences, and photos. The planned Android beta backup configuration includes eligible records and preferences but excludes Lapsha’s saved photo files from cloud backup and device transfer. Photos will need to be added again after an Android restore or transfer. Backup availability and successful restoration depend on your device and provider.

## Keeping and deleting information

Your notebook remains in local app storage until it is removed. Deleting an individual person, fact, or date removes it from view, but the record may remain in the local database. Use Settings → Clear All Data to remove saved notebook records, app preferences, and Lapsha’s stored photo copies. If cleanup reports a failure, follow the app’s instructions to retry.

Deleting data in Lapsha does not delete existing system backups or original photos in your photo library. Manage those separately through your device or backup provider. Restoring an older backup may restore information that you previously deleted. Lapsha does not promise forensic erasure of storage or backups.

## App diagnostics

Lapsha uses Expo Observe (EAS Observe), a service provided by Expo, to automatically collect and send technical diagnostics off your device. Expo processes these reports, and the developer can access them to investigate errors and improve app performance and reliability.

Reports can include app and build versions, device model, operating system, language, timestamps, screen names and navigation timings, startup and rendering performance, memory warnings, battery and thermal conditions, network connection and request performance details, and crash or error messages with stack traces. They also include a randomly generated installation identifier and session identifiers, allowing reports from the same installation or app launch to be grouped. These are pseudonymous identifiers, not your name or an account login.

We do not intentionally attach notebook names, facts, dates, photos, or database contents to diagnostic reports. Lapsha filters person identifiers from navigation reports and uses fixed messages for handled failures. Unexpected errors can include technical details generated by the app or its dependencies.

Diagnostics are enabled automatically in release builds; Lapsha currently has no in-app opt-out. Reports may be stored on your device while offline and sent when connectivity is available. Expo currently documents a minimum retention period of 60 days for metric data. Settings → Clear All Data clears your notebook but does not delete reports already sent to Expo or disable future diagnostics. Contact us with questions or requests about diagnostic data. Expo’s privacy policy is available at https://expo.dev/privacy.

## Feedback and platform diagnostics

If you contact the developer or send beta feedback, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, message, screenshots, and attachments. Please avoid including private details about other people. We use feedback to respond to requests and improve Lapsha, and retain it only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes. You can request deletion of correspondence by contacting us.

Apple or Google may collect diagnostics and provide crash reports or testing feedback through their distribution platforms, according to their settings and policies. These reports may include device, operating system, and app-version details. We use reports made available to us to investigate bugs and improve reliability.

## This website

This website is intended to be hosted on Vercel. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, requested pages, browser information, and timestamps, to deliver and secure the site under its own policies. We do not add advertising cookies or website analytics. Visiting this website does not give us access to your Lapsha notebook.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Lapsha changes. The latest version will be available on this page with an updated date.
